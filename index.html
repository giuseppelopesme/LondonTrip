Your call — enter the Tower?

Inside is ~£37 pp and 2–3 hrs; skip it and you gain an unhurried river walk and an earlier pint. Do it, and dinner simply shifts later — the Prospect serves till ~21:00 and is open to 23:00.

The river walk: follow the Thames Path east through Wapping to the pub (~30 min / 1.5 mi) — cobbled lanes, old wharves, and quiet river views.

Day 03 — Rods & West End · Sat 1 Aug

Farlows, Covent Garden & Soho

Food · £46 ppFarlows
The shape of the day

Start at the London fly-fishing institution the moment it opens, then let the West End unspool on foot: Fortnum's, Covent Garden, Soho and Chinatown, an aperitivo, and dinner. All within a tight, walkable square.

Getting there

Piccadilly line: Gloucester Road → Piccadilly Circus (direct, ~10 min), then a 4-min walk down to Pall Mall.

Transit in Apple Maps
10:00
Farlows — 9 Pall Mall
Est. 1840: fly rods, reels, waders, country clothing, and Chris Clemes' hand-built cane rods in-store. Placed on Saturday because it's closed Sundays (Sat hours 10–18).
Where9 Pall Mall, SW1Y 5NP · tube Piccadilly Circus
NoteNo ticket — just go in. Ask about the cane-rod room upstairs.
Mapfarlows.co.uk

Two minutes away: up to Piccadilly for a browse of Fortnum & Mason's food hall (181 Piccadilly) — a good spot for an edible souvenir without the Harrods crowds.

Day 04 — Day Trip · Sun 2 Aug

Oxford by rail

Food · £44 pp+ train ~£28, separateGWR direct
The shape of the day

An hour from Paddington and walkable off the platform. The order is set by Sunday hours: the market and colleges in the morning, the tower at noon, Christ Church from 2pm, and punting to finish. Book two things before you go (train + Christ Church).

Getting there

Circle line: Gloucester Road → Paddington (~13 min). GWR direct to Oxford (~55 min). From Oxford station, a 12-min walk into the centre via Park End St and George St.

Book before you go

Two quick bookings make the day: advance train singles (much cheaper), and a Christ Church timed ticket for after 2pm. Everything else — market, tower, the Turf, the Bear — is walk-up. Prefer a palace? Bus S3 from Oxford reaches Blenheim Palace in ~40 min.
